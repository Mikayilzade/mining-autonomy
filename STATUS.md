## Durable economics findings
- **Paid utilization remains the dominant hidden variable.**
- The closest compliant form of “bots mining simple online tasks” remains autonomous provider webhooks/APIs/nodes that marketplaces explicitly permit machines to operate.
- Low-variable-cost deterministic services are theoretically superior to LLM/API wrappers unless resale margins are proven.
- x402 is a payment rail, not proof of demand.
- Cloud compatibility must be explicit; Open Cloud is unusually strong because cloud-backed resale is directly documented.
- Small Linux hosting nodes can be more attractive than GPU networks when collateral/token economics and demand are favorable; Evernode is now a priority example.
- Stake/collateral must be modeled as capital with liquidity/opportunity/slashing or exit-loss risk.
- Azerbaijan remains a hard pre-CAPEX validation gate.
