## Durable economics findings
- **Paid utilization, not uptime, remains the dominant hidden variable.**
- The closest legitimate form of “bots mining simple online tasks” is usually a daemon selling a machine-readable resource: proof generation, RPC/API queries, indexing/data service, storage, bandwidth/relay, CPU/GPU capacity or validator duties.
- **ZK proof marketplaces are now one of the strongest primary-target clusters:** Boundless and Cysic both expose current machine-native work/reward paths.
- Boundless is especially close to a true machine-job market because the Broker can autonomously price/bid/lock/fulfill requests and revenue includes direct requester payments, not only emissions.
- Cysic similarly uses task bidding and proof rewards but adds token reserve/slashing economics.
- **RPC/data infrastructure remains concrete paid machine work:** Lava, SubQuery, The Graph and SQD now provide several current examples with differing capital barriers.
- Customer-paid or usage-linked revenue is stronger evidence than generic emissions, but demand must still be measured.
- Owned spare resources structurally outperform retail-cloud re-rental unless the earning market pays an unusual premium.
- Stake/collateral/license cost has opportunity cost, liquidity risk and possible slashing/loss exposure.
- Permissionless protocol wording does not prove every dependent wallet, exchange, cloud or off-ramp is available in Azerbaijan.
- Small empirical pilots should precede CAPEX.
