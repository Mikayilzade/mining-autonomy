## Run 015 — durable findings
This pass established a defensive anti-scam / anti-rediscovery layer and found several meaningful state changes.

### Current reward-path corrections
- **DAWN:** current Terms say Rewards are points with no monetary value and no guaranteed conversion. `REJECTED` as current cash income; future network economics remain watchlist only.
- **Nodepay:** current docs now describe monthly conversion of eligible points into Nodecoin ($NC), claimable to a wallet and withdrawable on-chain. Upgrade from points-only ambiguity to `RESTRICTED`; bandwidth-specific rates/environment rules still need normalization.
- **Grass:** current official material confirms bandwidth reward paths including Grass Tokens or USDC; July 10 2026 official Stage 2 notice says eligible participant rewards are being made available in USDC. Points themselves still have no monetary value and future rewards are not guaranteed. `VERIFIED` rewarded bandwidth, deterministic profitability `RESTRICTED`.
- **EarnApp:** live but explicitly personal/residential only; VMs, Docker, cloud hosting, personal/home servers and business/monetization-purpose devices prohibited. Server-native deployment rejected.
- **Pawns.app:** current traffic-sharing Terms require residential IP and disallow servers/VPNs/proxies. Server-native deployment rejected.
- **PacketStream:** current Packeter Terms (July 20 2026) specify $0.10/GB, $5 minimum cashout, PayPal and 3% cashout fee. Current cash mechanism verified; datacenter eligibility still unresolved.
- **TraffMonetizer:** current paid shared-traffic mechanism verified with $10 withdrawal threshold; Docker exists but datacenter permission is not inferred from Docker support.
- **Repocket:** current cash bandwidth mechanism exists, but current policy text prohibits VPNs/proxies/emulators/VMs for access; not a VPS-farm opportunity.
- **EarnFM Fleetshare:** supplier program remains a strong server/fleet lead: 20+ active IPs, KYC/supplier agreement/API key/user consent; explicit datacenter traffic rate in supplier docs. Consumer-app IP rules and supplier rules must be treated separately.

### Durable scam/anti-pattern rules
1. **No deposit-to-work:** task/job systems requiring worker-funded deposits/recharges to complete tasks are rejected.
2. **No pay-to-withdraw:** unexpected taxes/fees/deposits demanded to release already-earned balances are a major fraud red flag.
3. **No guaranteed mining APY:** fixed/high/guaranteed mining, staking or yield claims are presumptively deceptive until independently proven.
4. **Points are not revenue:** only current redeemable cash/token value enters economics.
5. **Role-specific ToS:** consumer, supplier, SDK, reseller and fleet programs must be checked separately.
6. **Docker/Linux does not prove VPS permission.** Datacenter eligibility requires explicit evidence.
7. **Referral revenue is a layer, not proof of underlying demand.**
8. **Cloud-mining contracts are counterparty/investment products, not direct mining infrastructure.**
