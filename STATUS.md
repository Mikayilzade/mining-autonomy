## Durable economics findings
- **Paid utilization, not uptime, remains the dominant hidden variable.**
- Owned spare resources structurally outperform retail-cloud re-rental unless the earning market pays an unusual premium.
- Provider revenue shares now give a useful normalization axis: DCP 75%, Paralon 80%, WorldLand 90%; Abakos sandbox publishes 88% host share for idle-mining conversion and a separate rental settlement fee structure. These percentages do not imply comparable net profit.
- Compare net $/GPU-hour, $/CPU-hour, $/TB-month, $/GB and $/IP across opportunities.
- Stake/collateral has opportunity cost, liquidity risk and possible slashing/loss risk.
- Device/license requirements can dominate apparent passive ROI.
- Curated supplier onboarding is common even in decentralized networks.
- Customer-fee allocation is stronger evidence than generic token emissions.
- Early/testnet projects can publish precise splits while still lacking liquid realized economics.
- Linux/server compatibility does not prove every VPS/datacenter host permits the workload.
- Small empirical pilots should precede CAPEX.
