## Economics / risk findings
- **Paid utilization/fill rate remains the dominant hidden variable.**
- Distribution is increasingly more important than the payment rail itself.
- Seller payout rails can be the practical geography gate: direct USDC, Wise, Stripe Connect, UPI and bank payouts have very different Azerbaijan compatibility.
- Platform-reported calls, providers, settlement counts and transaction totals are weak proof of real demand. Distinct buyers, seller receipts and repeat utilization remain the preferred evidence.
- Recent 2026 x402 research reinforces the need for replay protection, exact payment/resource binding, idempotency, spend bounds and wallet isolation.
- Experimental token/stake systems such as Conduit require separate expected-loss modeling for stake, slashing and token volatility.
