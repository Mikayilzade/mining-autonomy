## Durable strategy model
The machine-paid cluster now has four operational forms:

1. **direct self-hosted paid endpoint** — own API/MCP + payment middleware;
2. **marketplace/proxy monetization layer** — existing endpoint wrapped by a paid relay;
3. **autonomous agent-job marketplace** — structured request/bid/fulfill flows;
4. **build-once paid agent/data/content asset** — skills, packages, datasets, feeds.

These are implementation strategies inside the same underlying value source: owned software/data/content/service.

## Economics / risk findings
- Paid utilization/fill rate remains the dominant hidden variable.
- Catalog size, indexed services, raw settlement count and provider revenue share are not sufficient profitability evidence.
- Stripe-backed creator payout systems introduce a hard country-eligibility dependency.
- Wallet-native settlement reduces payment friction but does not solve legal entity, KYC, sanctions, tax, upstream rights or country eligibility.
- Azerbaijan remains a hard pre-CAPEX/pre-subscription validation gate.
