## Run 010 — important findings
Capital income is now classified by the actual source of return rather than the word `passive`.

### Traditional / securities families mapped
- insured savings and term deposits;
- brokerage/bank cash sweeps;
- money-market mutual funds;
- Treasury bills, notes, bonds, TIPS and floating-rate sovereign notes;
- corporate/high-yield/floating-rate/bond funds;
- dividend/index income, public REITs, mortgage REITs, infrastructure, preferreds, BDC/private-credit vehicles and royalty structures;
- P2P/private credit, invoice financing, real-estate/startup crowdfunding and revenue-share financing.

### Crypto / on-chain capital families mapped
- solo/delegated/pooled/liquid staking;
- restaking/shared-security yield as a higher-risk family requiring later platform validation;
- permissionless lending and curated lending vaults;
- stablecoin savings/RWA yield and centralized custodial earn as distinct counterparty families;
- automated yield/allocator vaults;
- full-range and concentrated AMM liquidity provision;
- fixed/term yield-token markets;
- basis/funding/cash-and-carry and other market-neutral-looking strategies, explicitly classified as trading rather than guaranteed passive yield;
- tokenized Treasury/private-credit/real-estate/revenue-share wrappers.

### Strong mechanism validations
- SEC materials confirm money-market funds earn from short-term debt/cash-equivalent portfolios but are not FDIC-insured investment funds; cash sweeps must be split into bank-deposit and money-market-fund mechanisms.
- TreasuryDirect confirms the main marketable U.S. Treasury families and bill discount-to-face-value economics.
- Ethereum official docs distinguish solo, staking-as-a-service and pooled/liquid staking; validator capital is exposed to offline penalties and slashing.
- Aave confirms supplier yield is variable and driven by borrower utilization in overcollateralized lending pools.
- Morpho confirms vault native yield can come from borrower-paid interest, with incentive campaigns and fees layered separately; official APIs/SDKs make automated allocator/integration products a separate BUILD-ONCE family.
- Uniswap confirms LPs earn swap fees for providing liquidity while taking price risk; concentrated liquidity increases capital efficiency but creates range-management requirements.

### New server-adjacent capital opportunities
- validator/validator-fleet operation;
- validator-as-a-service / delegation business;
- restaking operator services;
- automated treasury/lending allocator daemon;
- lending/vault integration product;
- concentrated-liquidity management service;
- cash-and-carry/funding bots using explicitly permitted APIs;
- broker cash/Treasury sweep automation where broker rules permit.

### Capital classification lesson
Keep four models separate in all later economics:
1. passive capital holding;
2. capital + node operation;
3. capital + trading/allocator bot;
4. capital + build-once fee-generating product.

No current APY/rate should be treated as durable. Azerbaijan/KYC/tax/access is intentionally deferred to a dedicated filter pass rather than guessed.

## Durable classification lessons
- Real-world verification (GPS, GNSS, cellular checks, cryptographic hardware, authentic camera/radio/sensor data) defines the commodity. Cloud simulation/spoofing is not an automation strategy and is excluded.
- Docker support on physical hardware does not mean a service can run profitably/legitimately on a cloud VPS.
- Distinguish customer-paid demand from fixed token-emission subsidy and model hybrids separately.
- Points/devnet units/in-app credits are not income until a current redeemable/liquid path is proven.
- A physical network can contain a separate server-native validator/storage/operator role; search infrastructure/developer docs in addition to consumer miner docs.
- High quoted yield is not an independent economic mechanism; always identify who pays and why.
- Tokenization is a wrapper, not a return source. Tokenized RWA yield must be traced to the underlying off-chain asset/counterparty.
- Automated strategies such as basis capture, LP management and treasury allocation are trading/optimization systems, not guaranteed passive income.
