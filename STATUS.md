## Run 007 important findings
- **Bittensor must be decomposed by subnet mechanism.** It is not one generic mining opportunity; subnets pay for distinct machine-readable commodities and performance.
- **Chutes SN64** is a strong autonomous GPU-server candidate: current miner stack automates Kubernetes deployment and workload management, with incentives based on compute time and inference bounties.
- **Omron SN2** creates a CPU/NVMe/network-heavy AI opportunity: miners perform verifiable inference and ZK proof generation, with published minimum/recommended server requirements.
- **Apex SN1** explicitly accepts autonomous-agent solvers and pays top-scoring competition solutions; conceptually close to the desired “bot does machine-scored jobs” model, but winner-takes-all competition makes revenue non-deterministic.
- **IOTA** adds a distinct distributed-training-worker family where miners process activations rather than rent raw GPU capacity or submit whole models.
- **Mainframe** adds scientific optimization/simulation mining with top-K rewards.
- **Allora** confirms a non-Bittensor inference/forecast reward market where consumers pay fees that are distributed to workers according to inference quality; reputers add a staked evaluation role.
- **Gensyn RL Swarm is not counted as current production income**: current docs say official swarms/hosted nodes are paused and current public market material still includes test-only rewards.
- **Prime Intellect** has a real external GPU-provider network, but current supplier onboarding is contact-based rather than clearly permissionless/self-service.
