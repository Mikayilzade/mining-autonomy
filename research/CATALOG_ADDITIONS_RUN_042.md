## Protocol layer
- x402: payment middleware/protocol, not an earning platform by itself.
- MPP: machine-payments protocol + service discovery surface, not an earning platform by itself.

## Durable classification rule added
Split machine-paid API monetization into:
1. own endpoint / own data / own service — preferred;
2. explicitly authorized reseller/enterprise capacity — restricted but potentially compliant;
3. ordinary third-party retail API-key/quota resale — restricted by default until upstream contract permits it.

## Economics warning
Catalog size, transaction count and nominal provider revenue share are not sufficient evidence of demand or profitability. Paid utilization remains the decisive hidden variable.
