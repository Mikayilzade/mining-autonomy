## Evidence cautions
- First-party reward and demand claims are not independent profitability evidence.
- UPGO.IO and several small/new GPU networks require production/liquidity/demand verification before promotion.
- A deployment guide without explicit open compensation does not prove an earning opportunity; this is why Relay third-party oracle remains unproven economically.
- Provider counters and marketplace listings are not treated as utilization evidence.
