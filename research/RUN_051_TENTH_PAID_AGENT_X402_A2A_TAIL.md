# Run 051 — tenth paid-agent / x402 / A2A tail pass

Date: 2026-08-17
Status: completed

## Objective
Continue provider-level discovery for legal, ToS-compliant paid APIs, agent-service marketplaces, x402/MCP monetization and escrowed A2A jobs. Deduplicate against Runs 041–050 and require an explicit provider payment path.

## New independent candidates

### Circle Agent Marketplace / Agent Stack — VERIFIED
- First-party marketplace exposes a seller path for paid APIs using x402/Gateway and USDC.
- Circle's July 31, 2026 guidance invites API providers to integrate and apply for marketplace inclusion.
- Current marketplace surface showed 40 services and 636 endpoints.
- Agent Platform Terms expressly allow automated systems/AI agents, with a responsible natural person accountable.
- Listing is curated and approval is not guaranteed.
- Fit: server-native yes; automation 5/5; main unknown is paid utilization after approval/listing.
- Compliance: sanctions and identity/compliance controls apply. Azerbaijan was not named in the restricted-territory lists retrieved in this run, but exact onboarding/product availability still needs direct validation.

### Synmerco — VERIFIED architecture/channel
- First-party marketplace supports service listings, jobs, negotiation, escrow, delivery and settlement.
- Official material explicitly describes agents watching jobs, bidding, delivering and collecting payment autonomously.
- Free registration/listing; current standard fee documented as 3.25% for $5–$100k transactions, with lower tiers for larger deals.
- Terms support crypto settlement across multiple networks and reserve the right to verify identity/eligibility.
- Fit: server-native yes; automation 5/5 in architecture.
- Caveat: real recurring external buyer demand was not independently established in this run.

### MindAgents — WATCHLIST
- Explicit builder path: register an ERC-8004 AgentPassport, list a service and per-call price, receive USDC when hired.
- USDC/Base escrow; public terms describe a 1.5% platform fee.
- Fit: server-native yes; automation 5/5 in principle.
- Kept WATCHLIST because public demand/seller-receipt evidence was not independently established and Azerbaijan-specific availability is not stated.

### Hunazo — WATCHLIST
- Agent-native REST API supports register/list/discover/transact without human intervention.
- Service/digital-goods sales settle through USDC escrow on Base; first-party page states a 2% fee on completed sales.
- Production surface showed 28 registered agents, 75 active listings and 0 escrow-protected transactions in this pass.
- Separate demo surface showed transactions but was clearly dominated by test/E2E artifacts, so it is not production-demand evidence.
- Fit: server-native yes; automation 5/5.

## Leads not promoted
- Agentbot/x402 colony marketplace: payment and marketplace primitives exist, but independent seller onboarding plus external demand were not sufficiently validated.
- ToolOracle paid MCP/x402 fleet: useful real-world evidence for the self-hosted paid-endpoint strategy, but not a third-party marketplace.
- SOL-402 and hackathon/demo markets: useful technical proofs, not production income channels without live paid demand.

## Durable conclusions
- No new top-level economic mechanism emerged.
- Four independent seller-capable candidates were added: 2 VERIFIED, 2 WATCHLIST.
- Provider-tail discovery is still not converged.
- Seller channels now need separate scoring for open listing, curated listing and self-hosted discoverable endpoints.
- Production seller receipts/distinct paid buyers remain stronger evidence than registrations, listings or demo transactions.
