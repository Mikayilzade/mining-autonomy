## Promotion criteria before implementation shortlist
A candidate should not move to implementation merely because it advertises a high creator share. Validate:
- real paid demand or active bounty/purchase signal;
- seller-country eligibility for Azerbaijan;
- KYC/legal operator;
- payout rail actually accessible;
- upstream API/model/data resale rights;
- minimum viable price versus variable compute/API cost;
- platform fee after promotional/preview periods;
- withdrawal fees, refund/chargeback/slashing risks;
- operational monitoring burden.
