## MCPize
- https://mcpize.com/docs/monetization
  - Subscription + x402 pay-per-call monetization.
  - Current standard creator share 80%; founding servers before 2026-06-10 grandfathered at 85%.
  - x402 USDC on Base; Stripe Connect for subscriptions.
- https://mcpize.com/terms
  - 20% platform fee for servers monetized on/after 2026-06-10; Procoders OÜ; Stripe Connect verification/tax requirements.
- https://mcpize.com/developers
  - Hosting, marketplace discovery, monetization workflow and platform-reported vendor payout metrics.
