## Evidence notes
- Public catalog counts, settlement counts and marketing claims are treated only as discovery/traction claims, not proof of independent profitable demand.
- x402 and MPP are classified as payment infrastructure/protocols, not earning platforms by themselves.
- Third-party API resale is not treated as compliant merely because a marketplace technically supports it; upstream contractual rights remain mandatory.
