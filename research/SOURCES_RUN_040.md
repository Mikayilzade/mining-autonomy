## Evidence cautions
- First-party usage/provider counts are not independent demand proof.
- Marketplace listings are not proof of paid utilization.
- New-hardware profitability must never be inferred from provider fee splits alone.
- Geography/KYC/withdrawal support, especially Azerbaijan eligibility, remains a pre-CAPEX gate where public evidence is absent.
