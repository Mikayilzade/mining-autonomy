## Strategy refinement
Add a distinct operating subtype inside owned software/data/content/service monetization:

**Demand-signalled production** — prefer marketplaces that expose bounties, requested-data boards, subscriber/usage analytics or call telemetry so an autonomous operator can create/update products against visible demand rather than blindly publishing supply.

This is not a new top-level economic mechanism.

## Azerbaijan payout note
- Stripe Connect currently lists Azerbaijan connected-account support as **preview**.
- Stripe stablecoin payouts can include Azerbaijan for qualifying US-platform payouts to individuals/sole proprietors, but this capability is platform-dependent and does not imply ordinary marketplace onboarding.
- Therefore Stripe-dependent platforms remain `VERIFY ONBOARDING` until the specific platform confirms Azerbaijan support.
