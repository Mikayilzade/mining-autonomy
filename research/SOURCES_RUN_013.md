## Evidence cautions
- Databricks proves commercial listings and MCP-server listings, but not a universal self-service per-invocation payout rail. Do not equate discoverability with per-call revenue.
- Snowflake paid-provider geography is explicit and currently excludes Azerbaijan; do not recommend geolocation/entity evasion.
- Microsoft Azerbaijan support is unusually clear in current official publisher and payout tables and should be prioritized in later implementation ranking.
- Revenue-share percentages and country eligibility are time-sensitive and must be rechecked before launch.
