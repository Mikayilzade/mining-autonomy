## Economics / risk lessons
- Paid utilization/fill rate remains the decisive hidden variable.
- Catalog/listing/download counts and first-party earnings counters are weak demand evidence; prefer independent repeat buyers, paid invocations, sold counts, funded jobs or attributable on-chain settlement.
- Metered MCP/API/x402/webhook execution remains the best conceptual fit for autonomous VPS-native earning.
- Valid payment rails do not imply liquidity; Hunazo is a useful explicit zero-transaction example.
- x402 implementation later will require replay protection, payment binding, facilitator-risk review and spend controls.
- Azerbaijan remains unresolved at specific-platform seller onboarding level and must be validated before CAPEX/subscription spend.
