## Durable lessons
- Paid utilization remains the dominant hidden variable.
- The closest compliant implementation of “bots mining simple online tasks” is generally a daemon selling a machine-readable resource/job result rather than automating human microtasks.
- Alternate vocabulary still matters: contributor/native/browser worker, agent compute, x402 compute, verified receipt worker, idle-device inference, peer USD, sharded/layer inference.
- Direct customer settlement and public payout telemetry are stronger evidence than generic token emissions, but real demand still has to be measured.
- Owned qualifying hardware usually beats retail-cloud re-rental unless specialty pricing exists.
- Collateral/stake/license cost must be modeled as capital with lock/liquidity/slashing risk.
- Testnet/devnet/preview rewards are not production-income proof.
- Linux/server compatibility does not prove every public-cloud host permits the workload.
- Azerbaijan must be validated through live onboarding/payout/geofencing and off-ramp dependencies before spending capital.
